# WildQuest outcomes

- [ ] Mission setup captures time available, environment, difficulty, and group, then generates observe, listen, move, photograph, and reflect tasks.
- [ ] The app detects a local/Ollama-compatible endpoint when available and otherwise provides a deterministic offline fallback that works in airplane mode.
- [ ] Trail Mode shows one task at a time with countdown, progress, and simple completion controls designed to reduce screen attention.
- [ ] Post-walk field note accepts a text reflection and photo attachments, then produces a title, observations, and one next-time question locally.
- [ ] The interface shows local-first privacy, connectivity, AI endpoint, and offline-mode status indicators.
- [ ] The explainer section communicates why open innovation matters and provides a clear demo path.
